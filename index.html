Questions, answered

Can I join from home?

Yes. We are starting with online sessions, so you can join live classes, diet guidance and story circles from home. You only need a phone. Our clubs will be in Bangalore. If you are not comfortable with video calls, tell us and we will help you set up.

Will online members be left out once the clubs open?

No. Classes and circles will be streamed from the clubs so online and in-person members take part together.

Is this a clinic or a care home?

No. Silver & Strong is an elder gym and day club for active, independent people. Our goal is a healthy, strong life: building strength, balance and mobility. Our team includes physiotherapists who design and oversee your programme, but we do not run a treatment clinic. For medical treatment, we will point you to trusted partner clinics and work alongside your doctor's advice.

I have joint pain or diabetes. Can I still join?

Usually yes, with your doctor's okay. We start with a check-in with our physiotherapy team, keep every exercise gentle, and adjust to what your body allows. We build strength and keep you healthy; we do not provide rehabilitation, so if you are still recovering from surgery or an injury, please finish that with your doctor or physiotherapist first.

Do I need to be fit already?

Not at all. Every activity can be done seated or at a gentle pace. We start from where you are.

How much will it cost?

We are still deciding, which is why we are asking you. Founding members will get the best rates. Joining the waitlist is free.

Where exactly will the clubs open?

First in Bangalore, around Vijaya Bank Layout, Bilekahalli and Hulimavu, after we have built the online community. The exact neighbourhoods depend on where most people sign up, so please tell us your area.

Can my family visit or take part?

Yes. Children and grandchildren are welcome at weekend events and festivals.
